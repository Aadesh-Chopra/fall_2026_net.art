Nothing But Receipts

This 50-word story was written by Haley Jameson and published on Fifty Word Stories .

The Linty Piece of Gum at the Bottom of a Purse

The gum lost flavor an hour ago. It was the last piece, lost to the bottom of the bag. To spit it out is to end something, or to acknowledge its end. So I chew and dig through her purse to find what else is left.
She’s nothing but receipts.

What I Notice

  • The story is only 50 words long.
  • Small objects carry emotional meaning.
  • The ending leaves room for interpretation.

Details That Stand Out

  1. The gum has already lost its flavor.
  2. The narrator refuses to let go.
  3. The final image is surprisingly powerful.

My favorite line is "To spit it out is to end something" because it turns an ordinary action into a reflection on loss.

The phrase "nothing but receipts" suggests that only traces and records remain after a person is gone.
