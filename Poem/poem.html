The Linty Piece of Gum at the Bottom of a Purse

The gum lost flavor an hour ago. It was the last piece, lost to the bottom of the bag. To spit it out is to end something, or to acknowledge its end. So I chew and dig through her purse to find what else is left.
She’s nothing but receipts.
